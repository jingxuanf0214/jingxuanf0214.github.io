Biography
My name is Jingxuan. I am a machine learning research engineer at Scale AI, where I work on agentic post-training. I received my Ph.D. in neuroscience from HMS advised by Prof. Rachel Wilson, along with a concurrent M.S. in applied math at Harvard SEAS advised by Prof. Michael Brenner.
Below is a list of research directions I have worked on, and you can reach me via email jingxuanf0214[AT]gmail[DOT]com to discuss potential collaborations.:
Post-training and alignment: NTP (NeurIPS 2026), RuleAdapter (ICML 2025), ENCORE (AAAI 2026)
Evaluation and benchmarking: HARDMath (ICLR 2025), CliniCARE-Bench, READY
Circuit dissection and interpretability: DiT Spatial Circuits (CVPR 2026 Highlight)
